Where the data comes from

The City of Alexandria publishes its Alex311 service requests. This site copies them several times a day, keeps the history, and links every case back to the official record. Reports sent from here go into the same City system as everyone else's.

What this site is not

Not the City, and not a way to reach it in an emergency — for anything urgent, call 911; for the City itself, call 311. Signing in here is not signing in to Alex311: an account on this site is its own thing and does not connect to any account you have with the City. For the official site, see alex311.alexandriava.gov.
