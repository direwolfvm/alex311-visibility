The name shows only to you — in the account chip and here. It is never on the public site: a score you choose to show carries no name, and nothing here reaches the City. Beyond that, this site keeps an account id and nothing about you, so a rating can be counted without a person being identifiable. Your email, where we hold one, is only used to sign you in and to link an administrator's invitation to the account you sign in with.

What we hold

  • Your list — the requests you sent from here and the ones you follow. See My requests.
  • Your ratings — whether a request was addressed, from 1 to 5, and any private note. Private unless you chose to show a score on its record page. Counted in aggregate on the Analytics tab, never with your name.
  • Sign-in details — an account id; your email where we hold one; the version of the data policy you accepted (—).

Nothing here is shared with the City. Requests you send from here go to the City's Alex311 with the contact details you type into the form, and those are purged from this site once the City has filed the request.
