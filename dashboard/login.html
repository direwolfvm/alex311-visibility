What this is. An unofficial mirror of the City's Alex311 requests, with a way to send your own from here and to rate what happened. Reporting from here is a test feature while it is evaluated with the City: every question on one page, the City's rules explained next to the answer that triggers them, and a warning when the issue has already been reported nearby.

Why it needs a sign-in. It can send real requests to the City, which dispatches real staff, and it keeps your list and your ratings — so it has to know which resident is which. It keeps an account id and nothing else about you.

What it is not. Not an official City of Alexandria service, and not affiliated with or endorsed by the City. This sign-in is for this site only: it is not your Alex311 account with the City, and it does not connect to one. The official portal is always available at alex311.alexandriava.gov, and nothing here is required to use it.
