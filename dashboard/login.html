Alex311 prototype

BETA

A test feature, open to a small group while it is being evaluated.

What this is. An experiment in making the City's own request form easier to use: every question on one page, the City's rules explained next to the answer that triggers them, and a warning when the issue you are reporting has already been reported nearby.

Why it needs a sign-in. It can send real requests to the City, which dispatches real staff. Access is limited while that is being evaluated with them.

What it is not. Not an official City of Alexandria service, and not affiliated with or endorsed by the City. The official portal is always available at alex311.alexandriava.gov, and nothing here is required to use it.
